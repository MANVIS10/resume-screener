# Opening B — House of Ved | Content & Communities Lead

House of Ved is a purpose-driven media house. Our goal is to provide a tangible experience of Dharma to a billion souls globally.

We use Vedic wisdom to help people positively transform their inner self, relationships, careers, businesses, and beyond.

We do this via social content, video content, community resources, events, cultural films, and immersive experiences.

* Accessible wisdom from the Bhagavad Gita and Hanuman Chalisa via the Kabir Aranya YouTube channel — amassing an audience of 85k

* Bite-sized Vedic insights via the Kabir Aranya Instagram page — growing to 170k followers

* An Immersive Dashavatar Experience — a one-of-a-kind immersion via cutting-edge storytelling, featured at a leading cultural venue in Mumbai for 8+ months

* Practical insights on Hindu culture via public events in London, Manchester, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and the Kumbh Mela
